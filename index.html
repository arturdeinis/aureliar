Members and guests

Before you apply

If something here is not answered, write to us. Every message is read by the founding circle.

members@aureliar.com
Who can apply for membership?

Anyone can apply. We look for people who bring something to the room: founders and operators, creatives, athletes, investors. Character, curiosity and discretion count for more than a title.

Do I need an introduction?

It helps, but it is not required. If a member has put your name forward, mention them in your application. Applications without an introduction are read with the same attention.

How long does the review take?

Usually within two weeks. Every applicant receives an answer, and most conversations begin over coffee in Riga before an invitation is offered.

What does membership cost?

Membership terms are shared personally during the review, once we both know it is a fit. There are no tiers and no public price list.

Can I bring guests?

Yes, at most evenings, and always by name. You vouch for your guest the way an ambassador vouches for a new member.

Where do you gather?

Riga is home. The season also travels: Jūrmala in summer, and a handful of evenings beyond Latvia each year. Venues are shared with confirmed guests only.
